Blockchain Certificate Verification

Issue, verify, and revoke academic certificates with an auditable on-chain trail.

This project helps institutions publish certificate records to Ethereum Sepolia, verify them from the original PDF or hash, and revoke records when something needs to be corrected.

Verify Your Cert Institution Login
Access Workflow
  1. Use an official admin@institution email address when requesting access.
  2. Your request will be cross-checked multiple times before credentials are approved.
  3. You will receive your institution login credentials by email after verification.

How To Get Access

Email rameez2838@gmail.com or manaalfarakh18@gmail.com from your institution’s official admin@institution email account.

That email identity will be checked carefully before any account is created, and your generated credentials will be sent back over email once approved.

What Institutions Can Do

  • Issue final certificates directly from the original PDF.
  • Verify certificate authenticity using either the PDF or blockchain hash.
  • Revoke certificates that were issued incorrectly or should no longer remain valid.

Public Verification

Anyone can verify a certificate without logging in. If you have the original PDF or the certificate hash, open the verification page and check the blockchain-backed record instantly.
